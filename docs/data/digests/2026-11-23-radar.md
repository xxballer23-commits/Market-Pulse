A moderate week: Rove and Bilt are the clearest sourcing leads, with a few smaller wallet-add perks and hospitality or dining tie-ins worth a quick look. Many items are issuer promos, not partner plays.

- **Rove**: Travel and rewards platform with its own points currency, travel portal, dining program and transfer partners. ([The Points Guy](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiW0FVX3lxTFBtZEhLX21WSUZ6NVhWZDJwOEhGZnYyUTRPa05XMjJGWjk0NzM1SEdBdDRodlRfbU03NHoxN2JHdzVZSWVfUWdkWl9kQXNhWE5HLXRvU0RCUnlFbTA?oc=5))
  - Signal: Launched an elite status program with up to every third hotel night free, plus shopping and transfer bonuses.
  - Why it fits: A fast-growing rewards ecosystem that already adds partners regularly, so it could slot into a premium card's hotel, dining and travel stack.
  - Angle: Rove elite status or hotel-night perks as a cardholder benefit, or Rove as a transfer or portal partner.

- **Bilt**: Rent-rewards and lifestyle card platform that runs a points program and frequent partner promotions. ([View from the Wing](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiywFBVV95cUxPbVBMa3JKbXNUbWcwQWtvblRfSmh1R2hYcG5JQ3JqQjBnOEFPdW5HaEpfYVlyVE9YNVJRQU96SzZTc1Q1Y3EzQllrMnVNUDB3eXZOYklGSGgwakRZM2tWZ29pdExlZU5WVC10R2pjcWR3TWxzWmxwdHJZcGpKVnBTWUcwRUJRTTliaENCYnc1TkxSTldJY08xMzY5UXZKRUx3RU1vdGNtN2UtbTZ3QWYwemwyeVR1VDlEbW1NNElRNGU2UU9iTnZCWnFSQQ?oc=5))
  - Signal: Reportedly seeking an $18 billion valuation (reported, not confirmed), while running a JetBlue Mosaic status match in its December Rent Day.
  - Why it fits: Fresh capital plus a pattern of airline status tie-ups points to an issuer-side rival and a possible partner pool to counter or share.
  - Angle: A tiered airline status or status-challenge benefit for affluent cardholders, modeled on the JetBlue match.

- **JetBlue**: US airline running a Mosaic status program. ([View from the Wing](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiwwFBVV95cUxNcEdXTExEdFBUZ0VwMmUyeDJTcHBnY29kUGhNSHl0bkxrLU1ISTZxOUFWZzZWQWxzcDgwQThUZGNON01VSnhTMFk5OEdvN0t5RkwtZF9xTE9qenl2NFdnWTZfeDBjNkhrQjFCZUdwVDlycXRhR3NLb0NmdF9CMGZhZkpBTEQ4TTlmdEEyNmkzNjJkWEpDRXpSaGR2QXZfN0tnNm95Z0ZITFRjY2NyVWl3Uk8yZ0VTYnkzeVdVcThodHd6dkU?oc=5))
  - Signal: Bilt is matching members to JetBlue Mosaic status for December Rent Day, with a fast track to keep it through 2027.
  - Why it fits: JetBlue is open to status-for-spend arrangements with a card issuer, which suggests appetite for similar deals.
  - Angle: Airline status match or challenge offered to qualifying premium cardholders.

- **GigSky**: Travel eSIM provider offering mobile data abroad. ([Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/u-s-bank-adding-new-benefits-to-altitude-connect-card-with-gigsky-mobile-data-in-125-countries/))
  - Signal: A U.S. Bank Altitude Connect GigSky benefit is reported as possibly ending 11/30/26; an update says new GigSky data in 125+ countries is being added.
  - Why it fits: Low-cost, high-use travel perk that travelers actually redeem, with an issuer already running it and the status in flux.
  - Angle: Complimentary eSIM data plan as a travel benefit on a premium card.

- **Resy**: Restaurant reservation platform, part of the American Express ecosystem. ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/resy-rewards-earn-an-additional-4x-membership-rewards-points-on-dining/))
  - Signal: Resy Rewards adds an extra 4X Membership Rewards points on dining for eligible cardholders.
  - Why it fits: Shows how a reservation network can be tied to card earn to steer high-intent dining spend.
  - Angle: Dining-reservation earn or priority access that could be countered with a competing platform tie-up.

- **Radisson**: Global hotel group. ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/11/23/radissons-first-paid-membership-targets-diners-not-guests-exclusive/))
  - Signal: Launching its first paid membership, aimed at local diners rather than hotel guests, with generous discounts.
  - Why it fits: A hospitality brand building a local, dining-led audience, which opens a path beyond traveler-only perks.
  - Angle: Dining membership or discount access offered as a cardholder perk.

- **Oura**: Wearable health ring maker. ([The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/deals/oura-ring-black-friday-2026-sale/))
  - Signal: A 25% off sale is being paired with Amex Platinum card credits, per The Points Guy.
  - Why it fits: Premium wellness hardware that affluent cardholders already buy with card credits.
  - Angle: Wellness or tech statement credit or card-linked discount on Oura products.

- **Revolut**: Fintech banking app. ([Executive Traveller](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiYEFVX3lxTE4zQWpQZTBhVGZtRnlMcndGZUFjX0ZPbGFlZWZCMG11NjluVzNvQ2ZLcXBycy1GWUp5MjZ1S2w2OUVDY19mSkhvUjlTZ2QyeERNRHVYSnJidjlaNkdFdXgtaA?oc=5))

  - Angle: Watch for loyalty-currency links that could extend to card-linked programs.
