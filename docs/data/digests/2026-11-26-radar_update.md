Two moves worth flagging since the last radar: Chase widening its transfer network with a new Asia carrier, and Amex tying a business-card credit to an AI subscription.

- **Chase / Cathay Pacific**: Chase added Cathay Pacific as an Ultimate Rewards transfer partner, covered by an enthusiast write-up. Cathay's partner award prices are rising about 20% on some routes and companion ticket awards are ending, per a separate report. ([Upgraded Points](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMihAFBVV95cUxNTTFxRTZnOURzM2JaMUtaWXBDZ0VHNFJvNXViSl9UUnRYaGk3dHNsNmp2Sml6Y2pLTDBHZmoyZHRnRXgtWkRhZmtDd25reWliVEJUR19lTkxtODhZUFE4MjBrT2VZNVJrSm1TS09fOEJiQjEwTHBpMlVvTTA5VWNyZVhuVUw?oc=5))
  - Signal: A top premium issuer is adding an Asia carrier to its transfer lineup, while the carrier simultaneously cuts award value.
  - Why it fits: Affluent travelers value long-haul premium cabin awards, and Asia carriers are a gap in our premium transfer lineup (take). The devaluation lowers Cathay's pitch value, so verify transfer ratios and award availability before using it as a benchmark.
  - Angle: Take: Check Cathay's post-change award economics, then scope Asia or Middle East carrier partners that could offer stronger redemption value than Chase's new route.

- **American Express / OpenAI**: Amex Business Platinum and Business Gold now offer up to $300 a year in credits toward ChatGPT Business, which now allows single-seat purchases at $25 a month ($240 a year), so solo operators can fully use the credit. ([Doctor of Credit](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/chatgpt-business-now-allows-one-seat-25-month-covered-by-amex-credit/))
  - Signal: An issuer attached a card credit to a fast-growing AI software subscription, and the single-seat change makes it usable by small businesses and solo operators.
  - Why it fits: Owners and founders skew affluent, and software credits are low-cost, high-perceived-value benefits that fit business card positioning.
  - Angle: Take: Test similar AI or software credits for small-business cardholders, and look at other fast-growing business SaaS vendors that would sell single-seat plans.
