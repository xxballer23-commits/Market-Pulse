A moderate week. The strongest sourcing signals are a fintech-to-airline loyalty link (Revolut and Velocity), a card-linked shopping offer platform (Rove), and a card-to-merchant benefit template (Oura via Amex Platinum). Several others are worth a look as counters or pairings, and the rest of the feed is mostly issuer refreshes and noise.

- **Rove**: Offer platform that runs personalized shopping offers paying airline-style miles. ([The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/news/rove-personalized-shopping-offers/))
  - Signal: Launched personalized shopping offers paying up to 25 miles per dollar.
  - Why it fits: A card-linked or loyalty-linked merchant offer layer with high multipliers maps to affluent shoppers who respond to earn promos.
  - Angle: Merchant offer feed inside the cardholder rewards experience, or a benchmark for our own offer mechanics.

- **Revolut**: Global fintech app with a consumer and premium customer base. ([Executive Traveller](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiYEFVX3lxTE4zQWpQZTBhVGZtRnlMcndGZUFjX0ZPbGFlZWZCMG11NjluVzNvQ2ZLcXBycy1GWUp5MjZ1S2w2OUVDY19mSkhvUjlTZ2QyeERNRHVYSnJidjlaNkdFdXgtaA?oc=5))
  - Signal: Customers can now earn and use Velocity Points, linking the app to an airline loyalty program.
  - Why it fits: Shows fintechs are buying their way into airline loyalty currencies, a model that could run in reverse for a premium card.
  - Angle: Airline or fintech loyalty linkage that lets cardholders earn or convert rewards into travel currencies.

- **Oura**: Maker of the Oura Ring smart wellness wearable. ([Upgraded Points](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMidkFVX3lxTE9UWmVoUXlFaEZoSmY3eE9uQnRKemhkd1pMOTVpRFUwTW5sbkVHb3VHNUdzUzJsdkY0T0hqR3dJY0p1LVVVSlZoMHduWTUyS3Q0d1hvVG9wcEJLNWZWaE9hc0E2bjlZenQycHMyVnNheVJIWndlZ0E?oc=5))
  - Signal: Amex Platinum cardholders can get the Oura Ring 5 for $99 plus tax through Nov. 30, via a card benefit tied to a Black Friday sale.
  - Why it fits: A wellness hardware brand with an affluent buyer profile, and a live example of a competitor card-plus-partner credit.
  - Angle: Wellness or wearable statement credit or member pricing as a premium card perk.

- **Resy**: Restaurant reservation platform. ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/resy-rewards-earn-an-additional-4x-membership-rewards-points-on-dining/))
  - Signal: American Express launched Resy Rewards, giving 4 extra Membership Rewards points on dining reservations paid with an enrolled card (eligibility limited, rollout unclear).
  - Why it fits: A reservation-linked dining earn is a direct competitor move in the dining benefit that affluent cardholders value most.
  - Angle: Counter with a reservation-platform partner offering bonus earn or access for our premium cardholders.

- **Hyatt and Delta**: Global hotel loyalty program and a major US airline loyalty program. ([The Points Guy](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMikwFBVV95cUxQY3BNMG4yU3p2M2gzbW5rcjdwZC1tRFRaQTRSUWNwamRyS3RGZTZBU3lCcmdMME4xRWd5YnozYlVpVjVvT0p4WmdYR2VnQ2Jpd0dmeV9CbnhYWUN3T3ViR0J6MElfMWN3TzBtUHMtdnVOUEJiWW80VE1sMGowalhfeDVVWE52X056bXFVeHFJQlZNcVk?oc=5))
  - Signal: Hyatt plans stackable awards, new Milestone Rewards and Delta perks, all slated to launch in 2027.
  - Why it fits: A hotel-airline loyalty tie-up shows premium travelers value cross-program benefits that a card could bundle or amplify.
  - Angle: Cross-brand travel benefit (hotel plus airline status or credit) that complements our card lineup ahead of the 2027 rollout.

- **Bilt**: Rent-focused rewards platform and card, reportedly seeking an $18 billion valuation. ([The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/news/bilt-rent-day-promo/))
  - Signal: Bilt's Dec. 1 Rent Day adds a JetBlue Mosaic status challenge and a redemption bonus up to 100%, while valuation talks point to a wider lifestyle and events push.
  - Why it fits: A fast-growing rival is using airline status matches and events to win younger affluent households, and its partner choices show where appetite is.
  - Angle: Airline status match or events and experiences access as an acquisition or retention perk, plus a competitor to track.

- **NetJets**: Private aviation provider. ([STT Info](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi4gFBVV95cUxORi1wZ2FtMVJ2cjJXRTBVVDU5ZC1CMUlnM0VuSGY5OFpEcGtaY0JhWU54ekRzSU1FYTdoVzYtbVRQZHJCeWFTZExNa2FMZWI5WG9Rbi1lRmJzQmgtUVVPU0Z4ZElwT0tCdWYteFJBZjRnRGF4RHo0dElZODdZYlpjMDl0XzVSVldJSEdtdU92VGJ4MV9GOVNfQWg2SkNONVdpS1hUMEllV2E4MWZUdFd5ejcwTmFmc0RPcGZsRWtFZER1Z3RiNmU4UGdyTmhnNzEzaUZRRVE4Qi1LMUhoQTFOUTh3?oc=5))
  - Signal: Named official private aviation provider of the Hahnenkamm Races in Kitzbühel.
  - Why it fits: A luxury-event sponsorship shows NetJets courting an affluent and UHNW audience, which fits top-tier card benefits.
  - Angle: Private aviation access, trial flights or event travel as an invite-tier cardholder benefit.

- **Selar**: Small-group Arctic expedition operator using a lower-emissions ship. ([Skift](https://skift.com/2026/11/20/selar-wants-to-make-arctic-cruising-less-like-a-cruise/))
  - Signal: Skift profiles its plan to make Arctic cruising feel less like a conventional cruise, with small groups and local expertise.
  - Why it fits: Small-group, high-touch expedition travel suits affluent buyers who favor experiences over status goods.
  - Angle: Bookable expedition experience or credit for cardholders, if the operator is open to a tie-in.
