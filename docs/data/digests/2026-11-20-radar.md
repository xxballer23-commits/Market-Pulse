A moderate week: a few real partner-style plays (Resy, Oura, Rove, Qantas and Cathay) plus one rising rival to watch (Bilt). Several items are copycat or counter plays, not net-new partners, so only the ones worth a team conversation made the cut.

- **Resy (American Express)**: Reservation platform that Amex owns, now paired with a reservation-linked rewards program. ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/resy-rewards-earn-an-additional-4x-membership-rewards-points-on-dining/))
  - Signal: Amex launched Resy Rewards, giving 4 extra Membership Rewards points on dining reservations paid with an enrolled card. Restaurant eligibility looks limited and rollout is unclear.
  - Why it fits: Ties card earn directly to booking behavior at restaurants affluent clients favor, and could be replicated with a non-Amex reservation or dining platform.
  - Angle: A reservation-linked dining earn or access perk with a booking partner, positioned as a counter to Resy Rewards.

- **Oura**: Maker of the Oura smart ring, a premium wearable. ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/oura-ring-5-black-friday-sale-99-tax-with-amex-platinum-card-benefit/))
  - Signal: Oura Ring 5 is $299 in an early Black Friday sale, and Amex Platinum cardholders can bring it to $99 plus tax through November 30 via a card benefit. Sale end date and credit eligibility are not fully confirmed.
  - Why it fits: A premium wellness hardware brand with a proven card-credit structure and strong fit with affluent, health-minded clients.
  - Angle: A wellness or wearable statement credit or member price, positioned as a counter to the Platinum Oura offer.

- **Rove**: Shopping offers platform that pays out in airline miles. ([The Points Guy](https://thepointsguy.com/news/rove-personalized-shopping-offers/))
  - Signal: Rove launched personalized shopping offers paying up to 25 miles per dollar spent.
  - Why it fits: A card-linked or miles-linked offer engine could supply merchant offers without us building each merchant relationship one by one.
  - Angle: Personalized merchant offers surfaced to premium cardholders, or a benchmark for our own offers strategy.

- **Bilt**: Rent-focused rewards platform and card program, now pushing into travel and events. ([View from the Wing](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiywFBVV95cUxPbVBMa3JKbXNUbWcwQWtvblRfSmh1R2hYcG5JQ3JqQjBnOEFPdW5HaEpfYVlyVE9YNVJRQU96SzZTc1Q1Y3EzQllrMnVNUDB3eXZOYklGSGgwakRZM2tWZ29pdExlZU5WVC10R2pjcWR3TWxzWmxwdHJZcGpKVnBTWUcwRUJRTTliaENCYnc1TkxSTldJY08xMzY5UXZKRUx3RU1vdGNtN2UtbTZ3QWYwemwyeVR1VDlEbW1NNElRNGU2UU9iTnZCWnFSQQ?oc=5))
  - Signal: Reportedly seeking an $18 billion valuation, and its December Rent Day adds a JetBlue Mosaic status challenge and a redemption bonus up to 100%. The valuation talks are unconfirmed.
  - Why it fits: A fast-growing rival building partner-funded perks (airline status, concerts, redemption bonuses) aimed at younger affluent renters, a segment partners will chase.
  - Angle: Partner-funded status matches or lifestyle events for renters and younger affluent clients, and a rival to track for partner overlap.

- **Hyatt and Delta**: Global hotel loyalty program and a major US airline. ([The Points Guy](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMikwFBVV95cUxQY3BNMG4yU3p2M2gzbW5rcjdwZC1tRFRaQTRSUWNwamRyS3RGZTZBU3lCcmdMME4xRWd5YnozYlVpVjVvT0p4WmdYR2VnQ2Jpd0dmeV9CbnhYWUN3T3ViR0J6MElfMWN3TzBtUHMtdnVOUEJiWW80VE1sMGowalhfeDVVWE52X056bXFVeHFJQlZNcVk?oc=5))
  - Signal: Hyatt plans stackable awards, new Milestone Rewards and Delta perks, all launching in 2027.
  - Why it fits: A hotel and airline tie-up signals where affluent travelers' loyalty is consolidating, and Hyatt has a strong premium guest base.
  - Angle: Complementary hotel or airline benefits timed ahead of the 2027 rollout.

- **NetJets**: Private aviation provider offering fractional ownership and jet cards. ([STT Info](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMi4gFBVV95cUxORi1wZ2FtMVJ2cjJXRTBVVDU5ZC1CMUlnM0VuSGY5OFpEcGtaY0JhWU54ekRzSU1FYTdoVzYtbVRQZHJCeWFTZExNa2FMZWI5WG9Rbi1lRmJzQmgtUVVPU0Z4ZElwT0tCdWYteFJBZjRnRGF4RHo0dElZODdZYlpjMDl0XzVSVldJSEdtdU92VGJ4MV9GOVNfQWg2SkNONVdpS1hUMEllV2E4MWZUdFd5ejcwTmFmc0RPcGZsRWtFZER1Z3RiNmU4UGdyTmhnNzEzaUZRRVE4Qi1LMUhoQTFOUTh3?oc=5))
  - Signal: NetJets was named official private aviation provider for the Hahnenkamm Races in Kitzbühel.
  - Why it fits: A premium event sponsorship shows appetite for affluent audience reach, which suits top-tier card flight and experience benefits.
  - Angle: Private aviation access or event-linked flight benefit for top-tier cardholders.

- **PayRewards**: Bill-pay fintech that lets businesses pay bills by card for a 2.9% fee and earn transferable points. ([Frequent Miler](https://frequentmiler.com/payrewards/))
  - Signal: Adding Atmos Rewards support soon, which is not yet live.
  - Why it fits: Turns otherwise uncardable business spend into points, relevant to small business and affluent owner clients.
  - Angle: Business bill-pay earn partnership for premium business cardholders.
