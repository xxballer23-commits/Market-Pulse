Airline-partner and points-redemption plays dominated this week. Big-issuer launch news was light, and I've said where that leaves gaps.

## Part 1: Premium card competitive intel

**Bilt**
- [Rent Day promo](https://frequentmiler.com/bilt-rent-day-up-to-a-100-bonus-when-using-points-a-statement-credit-jetblue-mosaic-challenge/): up to a 100% bonus on points redeemed for statement credits (normally 0.55 cents per point), paired with a JetBlue Mosaic status match. [InsideFlyer](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMimwFBVV95cUxNZlgwdGJISkFVMDZ1akpnQjhQdTFCcGVRRW42VUJOZkFTbmRELVpuZmUzWUxodHFORkZ1MnNURllKZHlyckhOYjY2UmpJazB6VExqMlRQZXU1M3JmQm9aUEFMY1oyQXdlSTAzY28xT3Q0bFdfR0NCT0o0N01jZHNCeW5pNW5IRDdmZDNqTFZneXlmTGxoMkZRU1hXdw?oc=5) describes the bonus as tiered.
- Partner: JetBlue. Eligibility terms and end date are not confirmed.
- Take: a partner-funded status match is cheap to run and lands well with frequent flyers. See Part 3.

**Bank of America**
- Targeted [Atmos offers](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/targeted-increased-bank-of-america-atmos-offers-125k-summit-90k-ascent/) rose to 125k points on the Summit and 90k on the Ascent. Partner: Atmos Rewards. Atmos is also running [double points on Hawaii flights](https://frequentmiler.com/earn-double-atmos-points-on-hawaii-flights-this-winter/) through March 10, 2027 (blackout Dec 18 to Jan 4).
- Read: BofA is paying up for acquisition on a premium airline co-brand.

**Chase and Amex: redemption sweeteners**
- Chase Ultimate Rewards is running [10 to 15% off select gift card redemptions](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/chase-ultimate-rewards-10-15-off-select-gift-card-redemptions-lowes-apple-uber-doordash-more/): Lowe's at 15%, and Apple, Uber, DoorDash and Choice at 10%.
- Amex has a [Membership Rewards sale](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiiwFBVV95cUxNRnBtVEtuMy1NMTBDdVZ4WTQ0NFBwY2RzaFNfX2c4UmFIOV9INVpreEFxc0M5VGdQUVVtN3k1MXpIRmxmUUNzMjRiWUJZckRwall2LWVQclEwSFFHUFU2ek05a3paM2hrMnZvaW9nSXNRSDc2STZDRnh2ZUsxZ1VpMTFReEcwVzI0Ujc0?oc=5) using 20% fewer points on some gift cards.
- Take: both look like brand-funded or margin-managed value adds. Worth asking merchants what they would fund.
- Chase also had a reported [data exposure](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMipwFBVV95cUxPZ2tTTmFoQ1dibGRSeDg5SWlQdThuY0pGRF9fc2xySXpMYWc0dDBEajZYNlBfeEpBVnBCM0sxTXJnbGJ4ZkVzcHk1eVk4OXNfN0NJbmFuX29fYTNqWFQtQVB1Wk5wVDhjcUVYMDlTUmF5aUVQWmdyRWVGWWNHNW5zTDhaTUdzdW9JTDhKSFd1YkpsY29lSko5Sk1vTllNTFNEOHI3VmhEVQ?oc=5) touching contact details for 16,000 reserve customers. The source is a low-grade aggregator, so verify before citing.
