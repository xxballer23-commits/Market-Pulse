## Part 3: One play to steal

**Resy Rewards (American Express).** Amex launched [Resy Rewards](https://frequentmiler.com/resy-rewards-earn-an-additional-4x-membership-rewards-points-on-dining/), 4 extra Membership Rewards points on dining reservations paid with an enrolled card. Resy powers it, and restaurant eligibility looks limited.

**Take:** Pitch OpenTable or Tock (or another reservation platform) on a reservation-linked earn for our premium cards, starting with a small set of NYC hard-to-book rooms. It would give HNW cardholders a reason to book through us.
