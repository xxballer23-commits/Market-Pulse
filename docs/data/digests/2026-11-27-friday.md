Wells Fargo-relevant news this week centered on policy risk (the 10% card rate cap push), a wave of Amex and Citi benefit moves, and Chase's transfer partner changes. Several of the biggest stories are not yet confirmed as binding.

## Five things that mattered

- **[Trump revives push for a 10% card rate cap](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMimAFBVV95cUxNdmx0a3BRZlliY1ZfTFgwZVZ6YkVsR3pjeXlZVzBaZ3lRUHg0eU5KZ0JsSnNlaEEwZjVicnB5TENFaWozcnhJcldQVUZkdkp2ZFZVTlV4S0ZVVU15QWJNYzF1WHAwYUxfcGdXeXBjZ004STJOWHRob2tPcXp4Z0JmSlpzeDBUbEtOUFh5eTdnc2dNeGp5XzFrRg?oc=5)**
  - The White House says it will work with the incoming Congress on a cap, and Trump is courting Democratic support ([PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/credit-cards/2026/how-10percent-credit-card-rate-cap-could-change-rewards-funding/)). It is a proposal, not policy, and the legislative path is uncertain.
  - It changes the planning case. Take: model rewards funding and partner revenue under a cap, since retail co-brand partners may be asked to absorb part of any cut.

- **[Amex Platinum's $600 hotel credit gets a new catch in 2027](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMitgFBVV95cUxOSzdWcDQzdUN1OFZVX2Y0QkpCVnFSbXM0cFNBSHM1dzJ0OUF2V3pubS14S29IcEpZQ0pRNWFOSDJXb3VCS0FGdW5XRThyWThlME5MUlNINVltMkJRalVrc3UzbTE5emtuUXRJdmh0Z3NWNUl2dzBNMTR3dFB0TlRXN2MyWktSYmdiVktpcUlDOW5fblplNDdLb0dDUlFUSVBib3JicEg5cHFacmxENGlYckZ3cjFIZw?oc=5)**
  - Amex is adding a new condition to the credit starting in 2027. The source does not say what it is.
  - It tightens the flagship benchmark. Take: a gap for rival premium cards to fill, so get the specifics before positioning against it.

- **[Chase adds Cathay Pacific, then Cathay raises partner award prices](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMif0FVX3lxTE9tdFNmMlhxSl8tM1pfZUlYTlpRM2p2ZWtmenJKWm10Ym5KSng0eFp3ajlvLXJHR2F5aVlvME9uVHZpZDVmUThYS2ttLUEzeHJQLWdNSDZ1bHkzXzQ0UHpHZWdGRlZuOGdIVGdxVldYZlNyUUZmaHRiVHFpMEdnOUk?oc=5)**
  - Cathay joined Ultimate Rewards, then about a week later raised partner award prices by 20% on some routes and ended companion awards ([Upgraded Points](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMiiAFBVV95cUxQZjkzWE91SFh6bFg2Z0ZzXzRBMHQ2Yl9qVUJHNGk5RllJTDNWdS1Wb2lYMXRRS0ppNEFwdUNjckpNczUyTG4zV0JacUN6SjY1QXE4aWFBVUliTFN4MUMzUzVvVEtPOEdpU01hRTZ0bmZ4MndOU0toSmFobF9SNy1TamhmeXpXMzFz?oc=5)).
  - It shows transfer partner value can erode fast. Take: check award pricing trends before pitching a partner.

- **[Citi adds ChatGPT to Citigold and links uncapped spend to American status](https://www.doctorofcredit.com/citigold-making-changes-to-200-400-annual-subscription-benefit-spotify-hulu-to-be-removed-chatgpt-added/)**
  - Citigold swaps Spotify and Hulu for ChatGPT in its subscription benefit. Citi also ties uncapped card spend to [American status earning](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMikAFBVV95cUxNVGoybUNwLWZFVWRCbGVYVm1CcDNCUDgyTWRzRlY0dUlIajk4WjN6dWx4cWJWWk5vTzAtTnNZbWxQb0VrV1ZkNUp5VDRKdjRNSHhOQnd1bjRyTEY0T210Z2lvd3l2NGJjV0s5TFh1SGlySkZlY0NuTEFRSmNnYjcxV2JwTkZyd1E1NGtxdlUySUw?oc=5).
  - Take: AI tools are becoming the new subscription currency.

- **[Amex Business cards now cover ChatGPT Business](https://frequentmiler.com/now-buy-1-chatgpt-business-seat-for-25-mo-or-240-yr-cover-with-credits/)**
  - Up to $300 a year in credits now applies, and single-seat purchases ($25 a month) make it fully usable for solo operators.
  - It is a template for software credits on business cards. Take: worth testing a similar perk.
